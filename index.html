The heat

The sauna runs on wood. Light the stove and let the stones heat while you unpack. Two tiers of benches, and the heat is yours all day.

Out of the heat and down the wooden steps into a quiet pool of the South Yuba. Clear snowmelt, granite boulders, nobody else in the water. Then back in.

The land

More to do
on 168 acres

The cabin and the sauna are what you book. The forest and the river around them are yours to use: say what you have in mind when you send your dates, and the host confirms what works.

Fishing
Brown and rainbow trout in the South Yuba. The general California trout season runs from late April to mid-November; bring a licence if you are 16 or over.
Hiking
Loch Leven Lakes trailhead is a mile from the exit: three and a half miles up to granite lakes. The Pacific Crest Trail crosses I-80 at Donner Summit, fifteen minutes east.
Dirt bikes and quads
Room to ride on 168 private acres, and a paved pad by the cabin to unload. Where you ride is agreed with the host. California requires a spark arrester on forest land and Placer County sets noise limits, so bring quiet pipes.
Motorcycle touring
I-80 over Donner Summit, Highway 49 through Gold Country, Highway 20 to the coast. Park by the cabin door, get the gear off, and warm up in the sauna after a cold pass.
Camping
Tents, vans and RVs on the land, by arrangement. Public campgrounds up here sell out months ahead; here it is one group, one booking.

Questions

Before you ask

Straight answers to what people ask before they book a place like this.

How many people can come?+

Up to fifteen. The price is for the whole place, not per person: up to 10 people, $500 for a day and $1,000 for 24 hours; 11 to 15 people, $750 and $1,500. The cabin sleeps fifteen, in bunks and a loft.

What should we bring?+

Food and drinks for everyone: the nearest full grocery store is in Truckee, twenty minutes away. The cabin has a kitchen with a gas stove, a fridge and a microwave. It is bear country, so food and anything scented stays inside, never in the car overnight. Bring your own bed linens and towels; firewood for the sauna and the stove is here.

Do I need a 4WD or chains?+

Not in summer and fall: the road is paved from the gate to the cabin. In winter the snow is deep and nobody plows: you park by the road at the gate and snowshoe the last two-thirds of a mile. On I-80, chain controls start at Kingvale, a few miles east.

Is there phone signal or Wi-Fi?+

Count on neither. Coverage nearby is patchy; AT&T is reported to work best. Leave the address and your plan with someone before you drive up.

Is the river safe for kids?+

The pool below the deck is quiet in summer, but it is a mountain river: snowmelt-cold, with current under calm water. Swimming is usually good from mid-July; in spring runoff, May to early July, the river is dangerous and nobody swims. In winter it is a plunge, not a swim: in, and straight back to the sauna, only on a day the river is calm. Life jackets on kids.

Can we make a fire?+

No open campfires in fire season, late June to October, here or anywhere in the Sierra. The sauna's wood stove is the fire you get, the cabin has a wood stove in the living room, and there are grills on the deck.

Can we bring the dog?+

Ask. Tell the host about the dog when you send your dates.

Can you hear the freeway?+

The exit is minutes away, so near the gate, yes. How much carries down to the cabin and the water depends on the wind and the river. Ask, and you will get a straight answer.

How does booking work?+

Send your dates and group size through the form below, or message us on Facebook. We reply with the dates that are free. A deposit equal to the rent holds your dates; the rent itself is due three days before you arrive. Nothing is charged on this site.

Who looks after the place while we are there?+

You do. Nobody lives here, and the owners are too far away to come and clean. You get a clean cabin and sauna with the firewood stacked; you fire the sauna yourselves, leave everything as clean as you found it, take your trash with you, lock up and leave the key where we tell you. Then the deposit comes back in full; cleaning or damage is taken out of it.

Can we cancel?+

Booked more than 14 days ahead: you have 7 days to change your mind and get the deposit back. After that, or if you book 14 days ahead or less, the deposit is not refundable.
