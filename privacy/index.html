Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

This policy explains what information we collect when you visit clubexit168.com, ask us about dates, or contact us; how we use that information; who receives it; and the choices you have. In this policy, “Exit 168,” “we,” “us,” and “our” mean Exit 168, the cabin and sauna rental on Rainbow Road in Placer County, California, and “site” means clubexit168.com.

Who we are and how to reach us

Exit 168 rents a cabin, a sauna, and the surrounding land on Rainbow Road in Placer County, California. You can reach us about this policy or your information by calling or texting 916-907-5365, by messaging the same number on WhatsApp, or through our Facebook page.

Information we collect

When you visit the site

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the IP address of every visitor for security purposes.
  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics and Yandex Metrica. Through cookies and similar technologies, they record the pages you view, how you arrived (for example, from a Facebook post or an ad), your device and browser type, your approximate location based on your IP address, how long you stay, how far you scroll, and which buttons and links you tap. We also track steps in the booking form, such as choosing a date or entering a group size, and taps on our phone number or WhatsApp link. The contents of the form are not sent to Google Analytics.
  • Session recording. Yandex Metrica’s Session Replay is turned on. It records your whole visit: mouse movements, scrolling, taps and clicks, and what you type into the booking form, including the notes field. Yandex states that it masks fields it recognizes as containing personal details, such as names, and never records passwords. Metrica also builds click maps, scroll maps, and form analytics.
  • Advertising tags. Google Analytics is linked to our Google Ads account with ad personalization and Google signals turned on, and the Meta Pixel is installed on the site. Google and Meta use cookies and similar identifiers to recognize your browser or device. The Meta Pixel reports page views, booking requests (with the price shown), and taps on our phone number or WhatsApp link. Google and Meta may link this activity to your account with them if you are signed in.

When you ask about dates

The booking form asks only for the type of stay (day use or overnight), your arrival date, the number of nights, the size of your group, and optional notes. It does not ask for your name, phone number, or email address. When the page loads, your browser asks our booking service on Cloudflare which dates are taken. When you submit the form, it sends the details above and the language of the page to that service. The service holds your dates for 24 hours, saves the request in a database on Cloudflare, and sends the same details to our private Telegram chat. It does not save your IP address.

You then send the request to us yourself. The site opens WhatsApp or your text messaging app with a message already written, and you decide whether to send it. On a computer, the site can show a QR code that opens the same message on your phone; the code is generated in your browser without sending anything over the internet.

When you message or call us

  • WhatsApp and text messages. We receive your message along with your phone number and any name or profile details that WhatsApp or your carrier shows us.
  • Phone calls. If we cannot answer a call, it may be forwarded to an automated voice assistant that we run on the Retell AI platform. Retell records and transcribes those calls, and an AI-written summary with your phone number, the time of the call, and its length is sent to our Telegram chat. If you would rather not speak with the assistant, hang up and message us on WhatsApp or by text instead.

After you book

You pay the security deposit and the rent by Zelle through your own bank, and we see the payment details your bank sends us. The person booking signs our rental agreement online through Smartwaiver, and anyone who rides off-road vehicles or hunts also signs a liability waiver there. Smartwaiver keeps what you enter on those forms.

Stored in your browser

If you switch the site’s language, we remember your choice in your browser’s local storage so the site opens in that language next time. The site itself stores nothing else and sets no cookies; the cookies on our pages come from Google, Yandex, and Meta.

How we use information

  • To understand how people find and use the site, and to improve it.
  • To show our ads on Google and on Facebook and Instagram to people who have visited the site, and to measure whether those ads lead to booking requests or calls.
  • To hold dates, answer your questions, arrange your stay, and handle payments.
  • To keep the site and the booking service secure and to stop spam.
  • To comply with the law and protect our rights.

Who receives information

  • Google (Google Analytics, Google Ads). Google uses this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • Meta (Meta Pixel, Facebook and Instagram ads, and WhatsApp if you message us there). See the Meta Privacy Policy and the WhatsApp Privacy Policy.
  • Yandex (Yandex Metrica, including Session Replay). Yandex is based in Russia, and Metrica data may be stored on servers in Russia. See the Yandex Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub, which hosts the site, and Cloudflare, which runs our booking service and its database.
  • Telegram, where booking requests and call summaries reach us.
  • Retell AI and the AI model providers it uses, for calls the voice assistant answers.
  • Smartwaiver, for the rental agreement and waivers, and your bank and Zelle for payments.
  • Your mobile carrier when you call or text us.
  • Public authorities, if the law requires it.

Some of these companies may store information outside the United States, including in Russia (Yandex). We do not sell personal information for money.

How long we keep information

  • Google Analytics keeps event data for 2 months and data tied to a user for 14 months; the 14 months start over each time that user returns.
  • Our Facebook and Instagram ad audiences include site visitors from the last 180 days.
  • Yandex, Meta, Google, GitHub, Cloudflare, Telegram, and Retell keep their own records under their own retention rules.
  • Booking requests, chat messages, and call summaries stay in our booking database and Telegram chat until we delete them. You can ask us to delete yours at any time.

Your choices

  • Turn off personalized ads from Google in My Ad Center.
  • Stop Google Analytics in your browser with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Change how Meta uses activity from other websites in your Meta ad preferences.
  • Stop Yandex Metrica, including Session Replay, with the Yandex Metrica opt-out add-on.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use a browser or extension that blocks trackers. The site, the calendar, and the booking form keep working without cookies.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Our site does not currently respond to Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signals. To stop analytics and advertising tags, please use the choices listed above.

California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (together, the “CCPA”), gives you rights over your personal information.

What we collect and share

  • Identifiers: IP address, cookie and device identifiers, and your phone number and messaging profile if you contact us.
  • Internet activity: pages viewed, taps and clicks, form steps, and session recordings.
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Commercial information: requested dates, group size, notes, and prices.
  • Audio: recordings of calls answered by our voice assistant.

We collect this information from you, from your browser, and from the service providers listed above, for the purposes described in this policy. We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information. We let Google and Meta collect identifiers and internet activity through their tags so that we can show you our ads on other websites and apps. California law calls this “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising, and it may also count as a “sale” even though no money changes hands.

Your rights

  • Know and access: ask what personal information we have collected about you, where it came from, why we use it, and who received it; and get a copy.
  • Delete: ask us to delete personal information we collected from you.
  • Correct: ask us to fix inaccurate personal information.
  • Opt out of sale or sharing: ask us not to sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • No discrimination: we will not deny you a booking or charge you a different price for exercising these rights.

How to make a request

Call, text, or WhatsApp us at 916-907-5365 and tell us which right you want to exercise. Because the site does not ask for your name, we will ask for details we can match against our records, such as the dates you asked about or the phone number you used, so that we can find your information and confirm it is yours. An authorized agent may submit a request on your behalf with your signed permission. We will confirm that we received your request within 10 business days and respond within 45 calendar days.

The site cannot tell who you are, so we cannot turn off Google’s and Meta’s tags just for you. The fastest way to stop that sharing in your browser is to use the choices listed above. For information that Google, Meta, or Yandex hold under their own policies, you can also contact them directly.

Children

The site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the effective date at the top.
